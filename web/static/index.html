VX.0S1 · Features

Voice that works like code

No cloud dependency. No locked-in formats. Your voice, your rules.

Global Hotkeys

Hold or toggle. Recorded + validated against Windows reserved combos. Per-app profiles so the same key produces a casual tone in chat and a formal one in docs.

AI Enhancement

Formal, casual, technical, concise, creative — or write your own prompt. Runs on Groq (free) or OpenAI. Multilingual with language preservation.

Q&A

Ask Voxy anything about your transcript. Pro+ feature.

Local History

SQLite on your machine. Search, export, per-entry delete, retention policy, off switch. Nothing leaves your machine unless you opt in.

Privacy First

API keys in OS credential store (DPAPI/Keychain). No analytics. Crash reports opt-in only. Your data, your control.

Real Installer

Signed Windows installer, tray integration, run-at-login, single-instance, verified update flow. No Electron, no bloat.
