We give a FAQ

What is Voxylis?

Voice in.
Text out.Voxylis is a Windows desktop app that turns speech into text using your own speech-to-text provider, with global shortcuts and optional AI enhancement.
A Windows desktop app that transcribes your speech and types it into the application you are using. You connect your own Groq or OpenAI key, or the web dashboard's browser microphone.
Hold your shortcut (Win + Shift by default) and speak, then release to insert the text. You can also start or stop from the tray menu and the main window.
It depends on the speech model you connect, not on Voxylis. Whisper on Groq or OpenAI recognises roughly 99 languages; Voxylis lets you pin any of 38 of them and auto-detects otherwise.
Formal, Casual, Technical, Concise and Creative, plus your own custom prompts. Enhancement is off by default and asks your configured LLM to rewrite the transcript.
Yes, to the provider whose key you configured — that is how transcription works. Audio is sent for the request and not stored by Voxylis. Transcripts are saved only in a local SQLite database, which you can disable or clear.
In the operating system credential store (Windows DPAPI or your keyring), never in a plaintext settings file. Settings only record whether a provider is configured.
